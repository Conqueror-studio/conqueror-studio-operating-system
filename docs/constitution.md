# Conqueror Studio Constitution

## Purpose

Conqueror Studio is a luxury digital atelier. We combine Renaissance-inspired craft, editorial clarity, and modern technology to create digital experiences that are beautiful, useful, and durable.

## Non-negotiable principles

1. **Craft over noise.** Every visual and interaction must earn its place.
2. **Design serves a real business outcome.** Beauty without clarity is unfinished work.
3. **Systems beat repetition.** Reusable learning is a deliverable.
4. **AI assists; humans remain accountable.** Do not present unreviewed AI output as final expertise.
5. **Truth builds premium trust.** Never invent clients, outcomes, testimonials, or capabilities.
6. **Respect client and audience data.** Use the minimum data needed and keep it secure.

## What “premium” means

Premium means intentional strategy, editorial restraint, strong performance, accessible interaction, dependable delivery, and honest communication—not decorative excess or inflated claims.

## Project acceptance

Accept work when the decision-maker is available, expected outcomes are legal and ethical, budget/timeline are credible, and the studio can improve the client’s situation. Decline projects that require deception, copied work, discrimination, abusive timelines, or inaccessible/unsafe outcomes.

## Decision authority

The founder is final approver for brand, legal, pricing, and launch decisions. In their absence, the team follows written scope, PDS, quality gates, and the least irreversible option; record the decision in an ADR.
